01 // DEFINITIONctf://what-is-it

What is CTF?

CTF (Capture The Flag) is a cybersecurity competition where participants solve security-related challenges to find hidden “flags”.
A hands-on cybersecurity game — not a theory exam
A collection of security challenges of varying difficulty
A way to practice technical skills safely
Often competitive and team-based
Solved in a controlled, authorized environment

03 // GAMEPLAYctf://how-it-works

How Does a CTF Work?

1

Choose a Challenge

Pick from a list on the CTF platform.

“Can you find the hidden password?”
2

Investigate

Look closely at the website • files • code • network traffic • program behavior.

3

Find a Clue or Vulnerability

Identify something unusual or exploitable.

4

Solve the Challenge

Apply the appropriate cybersecurity techniques.

5

Capture the Flag 🏴

Locate the hidden flag inside the challenge.

6

Submit the Flag

The platform verifies it and awards points.

⚡ More difficult challenge  →  More points

08 // ETHICSctf://ctf-vs-reality

CTF vs. Real-World Hacking

CTF

🛡 SAFE & LEGAL
Controlled environment — built for practice
Explicitly authorized — you're invited to attack
Designed for learning, step by step
Challenges are intentional by design
Flags prove success — clear goal
A safe place to build skills
VS

Real-World Hacking

⚠ HIGH RISK
Real systems — real users, real data
May be unauthorized — that's a crime
Can cause real damage
Vulnerabilities may be accidental
Goals vary — often unclear
Legal & ethical boundaries are critical
CTF = PRACTICE IN A CONTROLLED ENVIRONMENT
📌 Always have permission before testing a real system.
